# Decision criteria

What makes an offer worth my time. The `analyze_offer` prompt injects this whole
file, so the AI judges every offer against MY goal and MY priorities, not
generic ones.

## Goal
<!-- One or two sentences: the kind of role and company you are aiming for, and why. The prompt reads this section. -->
Grow into a backend engineer who builds machine learning features in production,
at a product company with strong engineering practices, within the next three years.

## Deal-breakers
An offer that hits any of these is *Not worth the time*, however good the rest is.

- Requires relocating outside Westland.
- The role is mainly manual QA or support, with no development.
- Requires a degree or certification I do not have and cannot get in under six months.

## Scoring axes
Score each axis from 0 to 2. The total (0 to 12) goes into the `score` field of
the offer.

1. **Learning**: will I learn things that move me towards my goal?
2. **Stack fit**: how much of my current stack can I use from day one?
3. **ML exposure**: is there real machine learning work, or only a mention?
4. **Engineering culture**: code review, tests, CI, mentoring.
5. **Product**: do I care about what the company builds?
6. **Conditions**: remote or hybrid policy, contract type, salary as a data point.

Salary is a data point scored on its axis. It never decides the verdict on its own.
